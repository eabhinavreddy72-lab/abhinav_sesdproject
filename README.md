## 📖 About the Project

CanteenGo is a web-based Smart Campus Food Ordering System designed to simplify the food ordering experience within a college campus.

Students can explore available food items, select their favourite meals, place orders, choose payment methods, and receive an order token for scheduled pickup.

The system aims to reduce waiting time, simplify the ordering process, and provide a convenient digital experience for students.

## ✨ Key Features

- 👨‍🎓 **Student Login & Registration** – Student-friendly access.
- 🍕 **Explore Food Menu** – Browse available food items.
- 🛒 **Food Ordering** – Select food and confirm orders.
- 🎫 **Token Generation** – Generate an order token after confirmation.
- 💳 **Payment Options** – Online payment simulation and offline payment.
- ⏰ **Scheduled Pickup** – Convenient order pickup scheduling.
- 👨‍💼 **Admin Dashboard** – Manage the campus food ordering experience.
- 🎨 **Attractive UI** – Modern white and blue university-themed design.
- 📱 **Responsive Interface** – Designed for convenient access across devices.

## 👥 User Roles

## 🚀 How to Access

1. Open the CanteenGo Live Website.
2. Register as a student.
3. Log in using your credentials.
4. Explore the available food menu.
5. Select your favourite food items.
6. Confirm your order.
7. Select online or offline payment.
8. Generate your order token.
9. Pick up your order at the scheduled time.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Digitize the traditional college canteen ordering process.
- Reduce long queues and waiting time.
- Improve the student food ordering experience.
- Introduce digital order token generation.
- Provide a convenient and user-friendly interface.
